# Code of Conduct

Contributors must communicate respectfully and keep technical disagreement
focused on evidence, safety, and the work itself.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, disclosure of private information, and
deliberate disruption are not accepted in repository discussions or project
spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove violating content and may restrict
participation when needed to protect contributors.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainers. Security
vulnerabilities follow the separate process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
